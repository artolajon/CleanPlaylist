About

Welcome to our playlist generator! We're a team of music-loving developers who created this app to help fans who just want to hear studio songs, with no remix, no live versions or any alternative version.

Our app allows you to enter the name of the artist you like, setting a custom filter and it will generate a playlist with all their songs. Our database of music is carefully curated, ensuring that we can generate the most comprehensive and up-to-date playlists for you.

We're dedicated to making our app as user-friendly and accessible as possible. If you have any feedback or suggestions, please don't hesitate to reach out to us. We hope you enjoy using our app!

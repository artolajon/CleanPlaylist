Privacy Policy

At CleanPlaylist, we respect your privacy and understand the importance of protecting your personal information. We do not collect any user data when you use our web app. We only get temporary access to your Spotify account through its API to create a private playlist inside your playlists.

Third-Party Services

We use the Spotify API to provide our web app services. When you use our web app, you may be subject to Spotify's terms of use and privacy policy. We encourage you to review their policies carefully.

Cookies

We do not use cookies or any other tracking technologies on our web app.

Changes to Our Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page. You are advised to review this Privacy Policy periodically for any changes.
